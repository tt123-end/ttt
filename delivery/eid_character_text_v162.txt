里猎手 / EID 角色特性（中文）

单发泪弹造成角色面板攻击力400%的伤害
每次攻击后进入换弹，持续1个攻击间隔
换弹期间受到的伤害翻倍
非主动付血型伤害实际扣到心之容器内的红心时，每次+1碎心
骨心外壳及其中填充的红心受伤不触发碎心
献血机、恶魔乞丐、献祭刺、诅咒房刺门等不触发碎心
巨人独眼式伪穿透：击杀后保留剩余伤害继续飞行
受到的负射速倍率惩罚减轻
普通射速软上限由1趋近0.6，不保底实际射速

TAINTED HUNTER / EID character traits (English)

Each tear deals 400% of the character's displayed damage
Reload after every attack for one attack interval
Damage taken is doubled while reloading
Gain 1 Broken Heart per non-payment hit that actually removes Red Hearts from regular Heart Containers
Bone Heart shells and the Red Hearts inside them do not trigger this penalty
Blood donations, Devil Beggars, sacrifice spikes, Curse Room doors and similar health payments do not trigger this penalty
Polyphemus-style pseudo-piercing: killing blows retain excess damage and keep flying
Reduced penalties from negative fire-rate multipliers
Ordinary tears soft cap approaches 0.6 from 1; actual tears are not floored
